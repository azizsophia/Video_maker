# Ep2 "3 Things That Will Speak For You" - pre-writing source table (verified)

Numbered-reminder series, Episode 2. Theme: three things that intercede for you
on the Day of Judgment. All three verified against primary sources before writing.

| # | Claim (as narrated) | Source | Grade | Verified text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Qur'an comes on the Day of Resurrection as an intercessor for those who recited it | Sahih Muslim 804 | Sahih | "Recite the Qur'an, for on the Day of Resurrection it will come as an intercessor for those who recite it." اقْرَءُوا الْقُرْآنَ فَإِنَّهُ يَأْتِي يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ شَفِيعًا لِأَصْحَابِهِ |
| 2 | Fasting pleads: I kept him from food and desire by day, so let me intercede | Musnad Ahmad 6589 (Abdullah ibn Amr) | Sahih (al-Hakim; authenticated) | يَقُولُ الصِّيَامُ أَيْ رَبِّ مَنَعْتُهُ الطَّعَامَ وَالشَّهَوَاتِ بِالنَّهَارِ فَشَفِّعْنِي فِيهِ  ("Fasting will say: O my Lord, I kept him from food and desire by day, so let me intercede for him") |
| 3 | Surah al-Mulk (30 verses) interceded for a man until he was forgiven | Abu Dawud 1400; also Tirmidhi 2891 | Hasan (al-Albani) | "A surah of thirty verses interceded for a man until he was forgiven: Tabarak alladhi biyadihi'l-mulk." Ayah shown: 67:1 تَبَـٰرَكَ ٱلَّذِى بِيَدِهِ ٱلْمُلْكُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَىْءٍ قَدِيرٌ |

Notes:
- The fasting hadith (Ahmad) couples "fasting AND the Qur'an"; to keep the three items
  DISTINCT (Qur'an is already item 1), item 2 shows only the FASTING speech clause.
- al-Mulk is a chapter of the Qur'an; framed as the specific "one surah most forget"
  reveal (item 3), distinct from the general Qur'an intercession (item 1).
- Honorific: the Prophet gets "peace be upon him" in narration where named.
