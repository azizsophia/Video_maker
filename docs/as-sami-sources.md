# "As-Sami'" (The All-Hearing) - source table (verified)

99-Names series, Episode on **as-Sami'** (The All-Hearing). Theme: Allah hears even
the whisper no one else can. Anchor = the occasion of revelation of Surah
al-Mujadila 58:1 (the pleading of Khawlah bint Tha'labah). Light name-load: only
Khawlah, Umar, the Prophet (pbuh), Allah, Madinah spoken. The wrongdoer husband is
left UNNAMED on purpose (his name was Aws ibn as-Samit; not tied to anyone).

- Surah name: al-Mujadila ("The Pleading Woman"), verse 1.

## The incident (occasion of revelation - Tafsir Ibn Kathir on 58:1)
- Khawlah bint Tha'labah's husband pronounced **zihar** against her (the pre-Islamic
  formula likening a wife to the husband's mother, rendering her forbidden) after
  she had grown old bearing his children. She came to the Prophet (pbuh) pleading;
  he had no ruling to give her yet. She kept turning to Allah in complaint.
- Jibril then brought down the opening of Surah al-Mujadila (58:1), hearing her and
  answering. VERIFIED (Ibn Kathir names Khawlah bint Tha'labah + the revelation).

## CORRECTION (QC fact-check, 2026-07-20) - the "above the seven heavens" line is CUT
- Ibn Majah 2063 is AISHA'S narration and ends with Jibril bringing down the verse. It
  contains NO Umar and NO "above the seven heavens" phrase (verified twice on
  sunnah.com). An earlier draft wrongly cited 2063 for a "Umar - seven heavens" line.
- The "Allah heard her from above the seven heavens" wording is NOT in Ibn Kathir; it
  appears only in Kashf al-Asrar (al-Maybudi, a mystical tafsir), attributed to Umar -
  not a graded/sahih hadith. Per the accuracy gate (no weak narrations shown as sound),
  that line is DROPPED from the script entirely. The video instead builds the climax on
  Aisha's verified-sahih line "whose hearing encompasses every voice" (Ibn Majah 2063).
- Do NOT reintroduce "seven heavens" with a hadith number. If ever wanted, it can only
  be framed as a tafsir report from Kashf al-Asrar, never as sahih.

## Adab / tone
- Celebrate Allah as as-Sami' - He hears what no one else can, and answers. Not a
  lecture on zihar; the emotional core is "Allah heard her."
- No depiction of the Prophet; no faces as subject; no other-faith symbols in footage.
- "Above the seven heavens" is quoted as Umar's statement within the sahih narration,
  not stated as an independent theological claim.
