Run 2026-07-07, three independent verifier passes, BEFORE any script was
written. Hadith verified against sunnah.com page text (via Wayback snapshots
of the exact URLs and api.sunnah.com - live site is behind a Cloudflare
challenge in this environment; the archived/API data is sunnah.com's own,
including Darussalam grades). Qur'an via the quran.com v4 API, translation 20

(b4/*.md) during the run.

because everything in the two Sahihs is sahih by definition; that is expected.

## Corrections absorbed into writing (the errors this gate caught)

1. Bridge: "thinner than a hair, sharper than a sword" is Abu Sa'id's report
   (Muslim 183b), not the Prophet's quoted words - labeled in-script.
2. 6:158 keeps its full clause "or had earned through its faith some good".
3. Grave: angels stay un-named (Munkar/Nakir is hasan-only); the one-question
   text is 1338, the three-question text is Abi Dawud 4753 (sahih) - never
   merged under one citation; morning-evening seat is 1379/2866.
4. Skin: exact SI verbs ("has made everything speak", "used to earn", "bear
   witness", "you assumed that Allah does not know"); Ka'bah setting cites
   4817/2775a.
5. Shade: "His shade", never "shade of His Throne"; left/right hands quoted
   from Bukhari 660; weeping man last per 660/1423.
6. Killer-99: land-command and "So he was forgiven" are Bukhari 3470;
   monk-as-100th and the scholar are Muslim 2766a; chest-lean Bukhari 3470.
7. Cave: single-spine rule on Bukhari 2272 (120 dinars, no rain, no crying
   children, seal-phrase per 2272).
8. Beast: all vivid non-sahih details cut; stakes carried by Muslim 158.
9. Dog: woman = Bukhari 3321 center; Bani-Israil detail = 3467; the
   reward-in-animals exchange attributed to the man's version only.
10. Zalzalah: both weak virtue/testimony hadiths cut; Bukhari 2371 is the
    hadith beat.
