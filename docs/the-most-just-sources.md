# "The Most Just" (al-Adl) - Banu Ubayriq story - source table (verified)

Standalone aura STORY short. Theme: Allah is al-Adl (the Most Just) - His justice
defends the innocent regardless of faith, even against a Muslim who lies. Occasion
of revelation of Surah an-Nisa 4:105-113. Light name-load (no proper names of the
individuals are spoken; only Madinah, the Prophet pbuh, Allah, Qur'an).

## The event (occasion of revelation - asbab an-nuzul, per tafsir)
- In Madinah, a coat of mail (armor) was stolen. The thief was Tu'mah (Bashir) ibn
  Ubayriq, of Banu Zafar (Ansar); he broke into the store of Rifa'ah, uncle of
  Qatadah ibn an-Nu'man. (Names NOT used in the script - "a Muslim.")
- To divert blame he hid the armor in the house of a Jewish man, Zayd ibn as-Samin,
  and he + his clan colluded to accuse the innocent Jew, arguing a disbeliever's
  word could not be trusted.
- The Prophet (pbuh) was about to decide against the Jewish man and censure the
  accuser - until Allah revealed Surah an-Nisa 4:105-113, clearing the innocent
  man and exposing the real thief.
- Sources: Tafheem-ul-Quran (Mawdudi) and Maarif-ul-Quran on 4:105-113; the
  narration also appears in Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3036. In the video the ROCK-SOLID
  anchor is the Qur'an itself (4:105, 4:112); the incident is framed as the
  documented occasion of revelation.

## Adab / tone
- Frame as a celebration of Allah's justice (al-Adl), NOT anti-Muslim or
  sensational. The lesson: Allah's justice is for the truth, not for a side, a
  name, or a faith. The wrong attitude in the story (a disbeliever's word cannot
  be trusted) is exactly what the revelation corrected.
- No depiction of the Prophet; no faces; no other-faith symbols in footage.
