# Why Ketabi Studio is stalling under 200 views - and the format to fix it
Deep-research findings, 2026-07-16. 25 claims extracted from ~15 sources; 7 confirmed
by 3-0 adversarial verification, the rest labeled plausible (verification was
rate-limited) or refuted and discarded.

## The diagnosis (confirmed findings)

1. **The "perceived AI" trust penalty is the core problem.** Consumer trust drops
   roughly 50% the moment content is *perceived* as AI-generated, regardless of
   whether it is (Raptive survey of 3,000 US adults, via Search Engine Journal).
   Stock nature b-roll + TTS voice + template captions is the exact visual
   signature viewers have learned to classify as "AI slop" in under 2 seconds -
   and both of our formats (documentary prophet stories, aura Quran translation)
   wear that signature. Production quality cannot fix this; the viewer swipes
   before quality registers.

2. **The swipe happens in the first 2-3 seconds, and that decides everything.**
   Shorts reward immediate retention in the first 2-3 seconds (confirmed).
   Plausible mechanism from TikTok-algorithm sources: each new video is tested on
   a small audience (~200-500 viewers); if most swipe early, distribution stops.
   That matches our exact symptom - videos stalling just under 200 views.

3. **TikTok weights rewatches and shares above likes/follows** (confirmed, Buffer).
   Passive "that was pretty" content gets neither. A video that answers a question
   someone has been asked in real life gets shared; an aesthetic verse card gets
   a like at best.

4. **YouTube policy now actively targets our template pattern.** "AI-generated
   content made with generic or unoriginal templates... without the creator's
   original, authentic insights" and "similar or repetitive narration with minimal
   variation across videos" are flagged as inauthentic/unmonetizable (confirmed,
   YouTube's own policy). This is a distribution headwind, not just a monetization
   one.

5. **The top of the Islamic niche is personality/character-led, not faceless.**
   The biggest verified winner (The Sunnah Guy, 2.9M followers) is face-forward,
   and his stated strategy is deliberately reaching a UNIVERSAL audience -
   crafting content non-Muslims also engage with - rather than polishing for an
   assumed Muslim in-group (confirmed from his own interview).

6. **Padding length hurts.** (Plausible) Completion-rate expectations scale with
   length; stretching a 15-second idea to 60 seconds actively suppresses reach.
   Padding the mercy video with holds to clear an arbitrary 60s bar was the wrong
   trade - length must follow the idea.

7. **TTS is not automatically fatal - but it is fatal in emotional storytelling.**
   (Plausible) Across niches AI and real voice perform about the same, BUT in the
   personal/emotional storytelling lane real voice wins by ~14 points of watch
   time. TTS survives best in educational/explainer framing - exactly the framing
   of the kids dialogue format, and exactly NOT the framing of cinematic
   emotional narration.

## Why the kids channel works while the main channel doesn't
The Mama-and-child format accidentally solves every confirmed problem:
- Opens with a HUMAN QUESTION in a child's voice ("Mama, why...?") - survives the
  3-second test because it sounds like a conversation, not a template.
- Characters create a "show" - parasocial connection, follows, "more of this".
- Educational dialogue framing - the lane where TTS performs fine.
- Owner-supplied unique art - no stock-footage AI-slop signature.
- Questions non-Muslims also ask - the universal-audience strategy of the niche's
  biggest winner.
- Ends naturally shareable: parents send it to other parents (share > like).

## The format decision (owner-approved direction)
**Bring the proven dialogue engine to the main channel as an adult version:**
a two-voice "questions about Islam, answered warmly" series.

Format spec:
- FIRST 2 SECONDS: the question, spoken, cold open ("Why do Muslims pray five
  times a day?"). No title card, no cinematic fade-in, no logo.
- TWO CHARACTERS: a curious asker (genuinely curious, not strawman) + a warm,
  knowledgeable answerer. Consistent voices video to video = the "show".
- TOPICS: questions non-Muslims and new Muslims actually ask (pray 5x, fasting,
  alcohol, hijab, why evil exists, what the Quran actually is, why Arabic).
  Keep the accuracy gate: every factual claim sourced, ayat shown not recited.
- LENGTH: as short as the idea allows. Target 30-60s; never pad. Longer only
  when the dialogue genuinely earns it.
- VISUALS: unique owner-generated art per video (the kids-channel pipeline),
  NOT stock Pexels b-roll. One continuous cozy/atmospheric background.
- CAPTIONS: word-by-word pop is proven fine - keep it, with speaker labels.
- END: a reflective button plus a question to the viewer to drive comments.
- The aura verse-card craft survives as the VERSE CARD inside the dialogue
  (the "THE QUR'AN SAYS" moment), not as the whole video.

## What we retire
- Documentary-narration prophet shorts over stock b-roll (as a daily format).
- Aura aesthetic-verse videos as a standalone daily format - the lane's winners
  use real reciter audio and real human elements; our TTS version reads as
  template. May return later as occasional pieces with real (licensed) recitation.
