## "3 things..." numbered series

### three-duas-answered - "3 Duas Allah Never Turns Away"
- Three whose supplication is not rejected (the fasting person until he breaks his
  fast; the just leader; the oppressed) - Jami at-Tirmidhi 3598, HASAN; corroborated
  Sunan Ibn Majah 1752. "Raised above the clouds / gates of heaven opened / by My
  might I will aid you even after a while" - verified. Fasting-person line tightened
  to "answered all the way until they break their fast" (dua accepted throughout the
  fast, per reviewer precision note). PASS.

### three-rizq - "3 Things That Increase Your Rizq"
- Istighfar -> rain, wealth, gardens - Qur'an 71:10-12 (Surah Nuh). VERIFIED.
- Taqwa + tawakkul -> a way out + provision from where unexpected - Qur'an 65:2-3
  (Surah at-Talaq). VERIFIED.
- Gratitude -> increase - Qur'an 14:7 (Surah Ibrahim). VERIFIED. (Saheeh Intl.) PASS.

### three-anxiety-duas - "3 Duas for When Your Heart Is Heavy"
- Dua against anxiety+grief "Allahumma inni a'udhu bika min al-hammi wal-hazan" -
  Sahih al-Bukhari 6369 (also Abu Dawud 1555), SAHIH. VERIFIED (excerpt of fuller dua).
- All-dua-as-salawat removes worry + forgives sins - Jami at-Tirmidhi 2457, HASAN
  (Ubayy ibn Ka'b). VERIFIED.
- Dua of Yunus "La ilaha illa anta subhanaka inni kuntu min az-zalimin" - Qur'an
  21:87 (al-Anbiya) + Jami at-Tirmidhi 3505, HASAN/sahih (al-Albani). VERIFIED. PASS.
