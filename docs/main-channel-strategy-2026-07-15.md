# Ketabi Studio — main-channel reach strategy (2026-07-15)

Why the channel stalled under ~200 views and how to fix it WITHOUT breaking any
rule (Islam-accurate, every claim sourced on screen, no melodic instruments, no
prophet depiction, adab). We change delivery, not substance. Research-backed
(Islamic short-form virality + royalty-free vocal-nasheed sourcing).

## The four levers (highest impact first)

1. **Hook in the first 1-3 seconds.** One striking, SOURCED line (~12 spoken
   words) + bold on-screen text at 0:00. No logo/title-card intro before it.
2. **Length 20-45s.** Completion rate is the dominant ranking signal; long
   premium beats bleed viewers on a cold account. Strong stories may run 45-90s
   only if retention holds. Front-load the payload; keep a new visual every ~2-4s
   (cut within beats via motion/Ken Burns).
3. **A vocal-nasheed / ambient bed** low under the narration (~-18 to -24 dB;
   `musicVolume` 0.10-0.13). This is the biggest missing emotional/retention
   lever. Vocal-only or daff/ambient — NO melodic instruments. Duck further under
   the deep title-card read; swell gently at the reflective button.
4. **Open loop + payoff.** State the loop in the hook, pay it off at the very end
   (the "button"). Word-by-word captions always (most watch muted).

## Hook templates (sourced, adab-safe)
- Superlative + ayah/dua: "One of the most powerful verses for anxiety is..."
- Open loop: "The Prophet (pbuh) named one deed heavier than a mountain of gold."
- Direct question: "Do you know what happens on the first night in the grave?"
- Number tease: "Three signs of the Hour that are already here."
- Mistake warning: "You may be saying this dua wrong every day."
- Contrarian: "You don't need hours of worship - this one line erases sins."
- Identity call: "If your dua feels unanswered, this is for you."
- Urgency: "There is one hour every Friday when no dua is refused."
- POV immersion: "Imagine standing on the Day of Judgment with one deed left."
- Story cold-open: "A man killed ninety-nine people, then asked if he could still
  be forgiven." (the Khadijah-style in-medias-res open)
Guardrail: the hook must be a REAL sourced line; soften superlatives to "one of
the most..." unless a hadith/scholarly basis is shown.

## Format archetypes that perform
1. Faceless story-time narration + literal b-roll (the Khadijah template) - best fit.
2. Ayah / dua spotlight (Arabic shown, English spoken, citation on screen) - high save/share.
3. Listicle "3 signs of..." / "3 sunnahs with huge reward".
4. Emotional revert / redemption story.
5. Text-forward + ambient reflective quote (muted-friendly, cheap, high retention).
Avoid green-screen / face-to-camera (off-brand, faceless model).

  Search: pixabay.com/music/search/islamic%20nasheed/

  nasheeds (voice + optional daff), royalty-free commercial, in-house (no

## Common mistakes that cap views
Weak/slow hook; too long, no open loop; NO captions (fatal muted); dead audio;
inconsistent posting; low-variation faceless output (YouTube July-2025 demotes it -
our one-distinct-clip-per-beat + cross-video variety rules protect us); hook
buried behind a logo; topics picked by "interesting" not view potential.
