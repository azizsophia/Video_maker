# Ketabi Studio — TikTok Creator Rewards Playbook (originality & quality)

**Read this before publishing another video to a monetized TikTok account.**

This is a *different system* from the copyright rules in `PUBLISHING.md`.
Copyright/Content ID decides whether a video gets *claimed or blocked*. The
**Creator Rewards Program (CRP)** decides whether a video *earns and gets pushed*.
A video can pass copyright and still be disqualified from CRP for being
"unoriginal" or "low quality." That is what has been happening to us.

## Why our videos were getting disqualified

Our product design — *"no stock, no generic AI, every frame drawn by code, AI
narration"* — is, under the 2026 CRP rules, almost a checklist of what the
originality engine penalizes:

| What we did | Flag it trips |
|---|---|
| Famous reciter recording (Sudais/Abdul Basit) as the whole audio bed | **Reused/duplicated audio** — the strongest *unoriginal* signal. The same recording is on thousands of accounts. |
| ElevenLabs AI voice **+** code-generated visuals **+** auto-captions — voiceover, visuals *and* structure all synthetic | TikTok's line is *"AI as a tool vs AI as the producer."* Fully AI-produced content is **ineligible**, full stop. |
| One background template reused across every video, word-by-word text-on-screen | **Templated / minimal original input / slideshow-like** → *low quality*. |

**Escalation is real:** content removal → posting restrictions → **permanent
removal from the program on the 4th violation.** Treat any disqualification as
one step from a permanent lockout. When flagged, stop posting that format —
do not "tune" it and keep shipping.

## The fixes, highest-impact first

The rule to internalize: **CRP wants a human in the loop.** AI may *assist*
(color, captions, B-roll) but must not *be* the creator.

1. **Record your own English voiceover.** Replaces ElevenLabs. This one change
   flips us from "AI is the producer" to "AI is a tool" — the single biggest
   lever, and the script is already written. Read it yourself.
2. **Add original footage** (even 3–5s): a talking-head cold open, calligraphy
   you write on camera, a mosque/nature clip *you* filmed. Original footage is
   the strongest originality signal available to a faceless channel.
3. **Demote the reciter recording** from "the whole video" to a short
   *transformative* component. Longer term: our own recitation, or a
   Creative-Commons reciter with self-generated word timings (forced alignment).
4. **Vary the visuals per video** so renders don't look mass-produced from one
   template (per-story palette, motion, and layout — not the same backdrop).
5. **Disclose AI** with TikTok's AI-content toggle for anything still
   AI-assisted. Undisclosed synthetic content is treated worse, not better.

**What does not work:** buying views/likes (originality is a content-policy
determination, not an engagement threshold), and re-uploading the same video
through the appeal flow. Use the Creator Rewards dashboard *review* option to
appeal, not a re-upload.

## Baseline CRP eligibility (so we don't waste effort)

- Personal account, 18+, in an eligible country, clean guidelines record.
- **10,000+ followers** and **100,000+ valid views in the last 30 days.**
- Videos must be **longer than 1 minute** and reach **1,000+ For You views**.
- **No** Duets, Stitches, Photo Mode, or padded-to-length reused material —
  none of these count as original.

## Pre-publish originality checklist

Before a video goes to a monetized account, it should be able to answer **yes**
to at least the first two:

- [ ] Is the **voice** a real human (ours), not a pure TTS read?
- [ ] Is there **original footage or on-camera** presence, even briefly?
- [ ] Is the reciter recording a *component*, not the entire substance?
- [ ] Does this render look **visually distinct** from the last one?
- [ ] If any AI was used, is the **AI toggle disclosed**?
