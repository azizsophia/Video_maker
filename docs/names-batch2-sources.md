## an-Nur (The Light) - scripts/stories/an-nur.json
- **Qur'an 24:35 (Surah an-Nur)** - the Verse of Light. Hero clause on the ayah card:
  "ٱللَّهُ نُورُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ" = "Allah is the Light of the heavens and the earth."
  Full verse verified verbatim (niche/lamp/glass/olive-tree/"Light upon light"/"Allah guides
  to His light whom He wills"). The narration paraphrases the imagery; only the hero clause
  is shown as the card.
- Pronunciation: an-Nur (النور) /nuːr/ + sun-letter assimilation -> "an-Noor". PENDING ear-test.

## al-Wadud (The Most Loving) - scripts/stories/al-wadud.json
- **Qur'an 11:90 (Surah Hud)**: "إِنَّ رَبِّى رَحِيمٌ وَدُودٌ" = "Indeed, my Lord is Merciful and
  Affectionate." Ayah card.
- **Qur'an 85:14 (Surah al-Buruj)**: "وَهُوَ ٱلْغَفُورُ ٱلْوَدُودُ" = "And He is the Forgiving, the
  Affectionate." Close ayah card.
- **Mother-and-child hadith**: Sahih al-Bukhari 5999 (Book of Adab) / Sahih Muslim 2754 (Book
  of Repentance), narr. Umar ibn al-Khattab: the Prophet pointed to a captive woman nursing a
  found child and said "Allah is more merciful to His servants than this woman is to her child."
  ADAB NOTE: the hadith wording is rahma (mercy), NOT wadud - framed in the script as an
  ILLUSTRATION of Allah's loving mercy; the Name al-Wadud is grounded in the two ayat, not this
  hadith's wording. On-screen citation "Sahih al-Bukhari 5999".
- Pronunciation: al-Wadud (الودود) /wa.duːd/ -> "al-Wa-dood". PENDING ear-test.

## al-Hafiz (The Preserver) - scripts/stories/al-hafiz.json
- **Qur'an 15:9 (Surah al-Hijr)**: "إِنَّا نَحْنُ نَزَّلْنَا ٱلذِّكْرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُۥ لَحَٰفِظُونَ" =
  "Indeed, it is We who sent down the message, and indeed, We will be its guardian." Ayah card.
  The word لَحَٰفِظُونَ shares the root h-f-z with the Name al-Hafiz.
- Supporting: **Qur'an 11:57**: "إِنَّ رَبِّى عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَىْءٍ حَفِيظٌ" = "Indeed my Lord is, over
  all things, Guardian." (not shown; supports the theme).
- Theme claim (Qur'an unchanged 1400+ years, memorized worldwide) is well-established and
  adab-safe; "huffaz" avoided in narration to keep name-load light.
- Pronunciation: al-Hafiz/al-Hafeez (الحفيظ) /al.ħa.fiːdˤ/ -> "al-Ha-feez" (no Wiktionary MSA
  entry; reconstructed from MSA pattern; the ħ and emphatic ظ are not producible on an English
  voice). PENDING ear-test.

## ar-Razzaq (The Provider) - scripts/stories/ar-razzaq.json
- **Qur'an 51:58 (Surah adh-Dhariyat)**: "إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلرَّزَّاقُ ذُو ٱلْقُوَّةِ ٱلْمَتِينُ" =
  "Indeed, it is Allah who is the [continual] Provider, the firm possessor of strength." (names
  the Name; not shown but anchors it).
- **Qur'an 11:6 (Surah Hud)**: "وَمَا مِن دَآبَّةٍ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ إِلَّا عَلَى ٱللَّهِ رِزْقُهَا" =
  "There is no creature on earth but that upon Allah is its provision." Ayah card.
- **Qur'an 65:3 (Surah at-Talaq)**: "وَيَرْزُقْهُ مِنْ حَيْثُ لَا يَحْتَسِبُ" = "And He will provide
  for him from where he does not expect." Ayah card / hook payoff.
- Maryam/Zakariyya (3:37, verified clean) deliberately OMITTED to keep name-load light.
- Pronunciation: ar-Razzaq (الرزاق) /raz.zaːq/ -> "ar-Razzaak" (qaf -> k per house rule).
  PENDING ear-test.
