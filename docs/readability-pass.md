### sirius.json - EDITS (1)

- `sirius beat 5` [cadence]
  - current: "Here is the layer astronomers add. Sirius really is the brightest star in the night sky... nearly twice the brightness of the next... eight and a half light years away in the constellation of the Great Dog."
  - rewrite: "Here is the layer astronomers add. Sirius really is the brightest star in the night sky... nearly twice as bright as the next. It sits eight and a half light years away, in the constellation of the Great Dog."
  - why: three separate stats (brightness, comparison, distance/location) are crammed into one ellipsis-chained run-on that jumps topic mid-sentence; splitting into two sentences lands each fact cleanly. All figures preserved.
